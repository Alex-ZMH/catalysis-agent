This is a read-only inventory for the catalysis-agent roadmap. It distinguishes checked-in solver code, historical acceptance evidence, and engine files present on V100. No solver, historical acceptance, or checksum verification was run.

- **Studio** means `D:/project/E-PAO/mlcc-studio-main/mlcc-studio-main/runtime/mlcc-agent/modules`. Paths prefixed by a Studio module name are relative to this root. The host bundle manifest, Python metadata, and shared scientific package are one level above it.

## RVE first — static effective-material tensors

Studio `rve/module.json` registers four queued DOLFINx operations at module version 0.1.5.

| Operation | Physical problem | Main output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `homogenize_electrostatics_2d` | True 2D linear electrostatics with two orthogonal unit electric-field loads | Full 2 × 2 relative permittivity tensor |
| `homogenize_electrostatics_3d` | True 3D linear electrostatics with three orthogonal unit electric-field loads | Full 3 × 3 relative permittivity tensor |
| `homogenize_thermal_2d` | True 2D steady conduction with two orthogonal unit negative temperature gradients | Full 2 × 2 conductivity tensor in W/(m K) |
| `homogenize_thermal_3d` | True 3D steady conduction with three orthogonal unit negative temperature gradients | Full 3 × 3 conductivity tensor in W/(m K) |

**Inputs.** The operation-specific schemas in `rve/schemas/` require `geometry`, `normalized_h`, `materials`, `boundary_condition` and `pore_model`. The 2D cell is square and the 3D cell can be rectangular. Physical lengths and material coefficients use explicit value, unit and source records. Geometry uses dimensionless fractions, radii and widths. Material rows must cover all region tags exactly and supply a positive scalar or a full symmetric positive-definite tensor. A crystal orientation label does not rotate the constitutive tensor. Admitted geometries include homogeneous, axis-aligned layers, core–shell and polycrystal in both dimensions; the 2D-only cases include rotated layers at a fixed 45° normal, circle and ellipse; sphere and ellipsoid are 3D. Explicit pore material is required for electrostatics. Thermal `insulating_cavity` omits pore cells from the solve but retains pore identity and averages over the full cell. The public 3D polycrystal contract requires zero grain-boundary and shell widths. In 2D a zero grain-boundary width omits the grain-boundary phase, while positive thin layers still require adequate resolution.

**Implementation.** `rve/science_entry.py` delegates to `rve_module/science.py:compute`, which selects `kernel2d` or `kernel3d`. Each implements `microstructure.build`, `rve_mesh.create`, `physics.constraints`/`elliptic`, `homogenization.homogenize` and `verify.check`. Gmsh builds the normalized CAD mesh. The physical solve uses P1 finite elements on SI coordinates, periodic DOF equivalence and a zero-mean fluctuation, or affine Dirichlet data for comparison. Unit loads are 1 V/m or 1 K/m and the thermal reference is 300 K. Per-load native-field readback reconstructs gradients, fluxes, work and periodic constraints. DG0 flux jumps are diagnostic and weak anti-periodic reactions do not establish pointwise flux conservation.

**Outputs and limits.** `effective_material.json` records full tensors, units, phase fractions, region/material maps, load responses and applicability. `coordinates.json`, `readback.json`, mesh/provenance diagnostics, and per-direction VTU/XDMF/HDF5/NPZ preserve fields and evidence. The 0.1.5 mesh count is advisory, with positive mesh size and the geometry, thin-layer and quality checks still operative. `tests/test_admission.py` covers zero-width material coverage, resolution, and soft limits. `tests/run_native_regression.py` is an opt-in native driver. The dated `MESH_COUNT_ACCEPTANCE.json` records 2D and 3D electrostatics below the old mesh-size floors. `ACCEPTANCE.md` records earlier electrostatic and thermal native cases, with incomplete geometry coverage and no general material calibration, statistical representativity or device qualification. The source exposes no elastic, piezoelectric, nonlinear, AC or transient homogenization.

**Transfer candidate.** Start with the effective-material and coordinate contracts, a homogeneous and layered analytic oracle, and the corresponding output/quality adapter. Keep the periodic mesh and DOF pairing plus independent readback together if the kernel is later adopted. Preserve tensor dimension, basis, units and source IDs. A 2D effective tensor or scalar fixture cannot supply a missing third direction or a device-ready material card.

## FEM second — 2D device solvers

Studio `fem/module.json` registers eight queued operations at version 0.4.3. Each pair uses the same native solver. The quantity variants normalize explicit value, unit and source records through the shared host unit core and retain the raw input plus SI audit. The SI variants retain their existing strict schemas.

| SI operation and quantity variant | Model and required inputs | Main outputs and scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `electrostatics_2d`, `electrostatics_2d_quantities` | Parameterized cross-section, explicit out-of-plane depth, uniform linear isotropic relative permittivity, ideal electrode potentials, mesh and output settings. Natural external insulation. | Potential, electric field, terminal charge, stored energy, energy/flux capacitance. Electrode cavities exclude the metal. |
| `electrothermal_2d`, `electrothermal_2d_quantities` | Geometry/depth, mesh, material and source records, DC/lossless or prescribed AC loss, frequency and voltage convention, and mandatory left/right cooling. | Real potential, electric field and power, followed by one-way steady ceramic/Ni temperature, heat flux, thermal balance and thermal resistance. |
| `ac_eqs_2d`, `ac_eqs_2d_quantities` | Positive frequency, nonzero complex voltage difference with RMS/peak convention, uniform or half-height layered dielectric, loss parameters, geometry/depth and optional steady cooling. | Mixed real/imaginary P1 electroquasistatic potential, complex Y/Z, parallel capacitance Im(Y)/ω, conductive/dielectric losses and optional temperature. Ideal Ni electrodes have no modeled dissipation. |
| `electromagnetic_2d`, `electromagnetic_2d_quantities` | Positive frequency, explicit closed guided port and nonzero RMS current, finite-conducting Ni, ceramic, lossless air, fixed μr=1, geometry/depth and optional cooling. | 2D Ex/Ey/Hz fields, port Y/Z, regional loss and electric/magnetic energies, energy-equivalent C/L and optional temperature. Feed/return energy is included in L. |

**Implementation.** The standard-library host adapter uses `fem/science_entry.py` to dispatch to `device_fem/fenicsx.py:run` for electrostatics and `device_fem/extended/runner.py:run` for extended cases. Extended implementation is split across `thermal_core.py` (tagged Gmsh geometry, electric loss, cell transfer and thermal solve), `ac_solver.py` (coupled real/imaginary variational form) and `em_geometry.py`/`em_solver.py` (solid/air regions, guided port and frequency-domain Maxwell). It publishes the static or extended schema, diagnostics plus registered VTU/XDMF/HDF5, and a heat-transfer cell map where used. Optional images derive from the computed fields. Keep signed terminal flux, weak/discrete balance and raw gradient flux as separate diagnostics. DC energy capacitance, AC parallel capacitance and EM energy-equivalent capacitance have different definitions.

**Evidence and limits.** `tests/test_mesh_recommendation.py` exercises preflight and soft count warnings for all eight operations, retained geometry/material checks, and both deployment profiles. The opt-in `tests/run_mesh_recommendation_native.py` and recorded `MESH_COUNT_ACCEPTANCE.json` cover all eight native operations plus high-cell-count cases. `EXTENDED_ACCEPTANCE.md` and `EXTENDED_UNITS_ACCEPTANCE.md` provide earlier scoped method and SI/quantity equivalence evidence. None of the above constitutes calibrated device applicability or an independent device reference. The implemented thermal coupling is one-way and steady. There is no 3D packaging, open-domain radiation, temperature feedback, commercial ESR/ESL/SRF qualification or COMSOL solve. The atomistic package's `src/mlcc/comsol_export.py:export_comsol_material_package` exports material data and is a separate interface.

**Transfer candidate.** Adopt the static result quantities, geometry/BC schema, and analytic capacitor checks first. Make extended electrothermal, EQS and EM separate deliverables with explicit port, phasor, heat and depth conventions. The existing catalysis-agent `packages/science/vtu_reader.py` and `field_probe.py` offer an import boundary for a compatible serial VTU. Check actual piece count, cell/array encoding, size budgets, and point/cell unit metadata before relying on it. Labels must be supplied for unlabeled arrays. A field or region projection should be explicit. Keep bulk native field files as artifacts rather than importing them into every result summary.
